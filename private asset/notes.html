First-Timer's Sales Playbook

The "Yes Now" Subscription Strategy

The "Newbie" Flow The Strategy

Step 1: The Warm-Up (2 mins)

  • "How has business been? Are you getting many travelers from hotels lately?"
  • Wait for them to complain about low bookings or bad tech. This is your "Hook."

Step 2: The Presentation (5 mins)

  • Follow the Slides. Don't rush. Let them look at the visuals.
  • When you hit Slide 2 (The Crisis), look them in the eye and say: "This is why the old ways aren't working anymore."

Step 3: The "Wow" (Live Demo)

  • Open the site. Show them how fast it is. Show them the WhatsApp button.

Step 4: The Close (The "No-Brainer")

  • Show Slide 7. Say: "It's just 1,500 pesos per month. That's one massage booking per month to pay for the whole site."

Step 5: The "Fast Action Bonus" (The Yes)

  • "If you say yes now, I'll also design your full Brand Kit—Logo, Typography, and Social Assets—totally for free. I usually charge extra for this."

Slide 1: The Vision Intro

"Thank you for your time. Today, we aren't just talking about a new website. We are talking about future-proofing your business in an era where the internet is changing faster than ever."

Key Points

  • Set a confident, high-level tone.
  • Frame the project as a "Digital Sanctuary," not just a site.
  • Mention the goal: "Market Dominance."

Slide 2: The AI Crisis The Hook

"Google has changed. Their new AI Snapshots are now answering guests directly. If your current site isn't architected for this, you are becoming invisible to the high-value traveler."

Key Points

  • Explain SGE (Search Generative Experience) simply.
  • Highlight that competitors are still using outdated "2020" tactics.
  • Emphasize the "Conversion Leak" (losing money).

Slide 3: Quiet Luxury The Brand

"Price is only an issue in the absence of value. We've built a visual identity that screams authority, allowing you to charge the premium rates your service deserves."

Key Points

  • Contrast "Generic/Cluttered" vs. "Elite/Restorative."
  • Focus on "Building Trust" with professional guests.
  • Explain the "Midnight Navy" palette as a luxury psychological trigger.

Slide 4: The Strategy The Tech

"We don't just target keywords; we target Intent. We've built in the 'Expert signals' that Google's algorithm now requires for 2026 ranking."

Key Points

  • Mention "Structured Data" (the AI's food).
  • Hyper-local targeting (Angeles, Clark, Balibago).
  • E-E-A-T (Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).

Slide 5: The Funnel The ROI

"A beautiful site is useless if it doesn't sell. Our booking engine is friction-free, moving a guest from curiosity to a WhatsApp lead in under 30 seconds."

Key Points

  • Focus on the "30-second" booking speed.
  • Highlight the "Direct to WhatsApp" efficiency.
  • Mention that it works 24/7, capturing leads while they sleep.

Slide 6: The Live Demo The Proof

"Before we talk numbers, I want you to feel the experience yourself. Let’s look at the live sanctuary we’ve built for you."

Key Points

  • Click the button to show the actual index.html.
  • Let them see the mobile responsiveness.
  • Say nothing for 10 seconds—let the design speak.

Slide 7: The Investment The Close

"We aren't just selling a one-off project. For ₱1,500 a month, we are partners in your growth. But, if you say yes now, I have something special for you."

Key Points

  • Show the ₱1,500 price. Explain it's a subscription for "Zero Maintenance."
  • Point to the 'Fast Action Bonus' badge.
  • Click 'Claim Fast Action Bonus' to move to the next slide.

Slide 8: The Reveal The Gift

"I’ve already designed your new elite brand identity. You get the Primary Logo, the Social Media Kit, and the Business Card designs for free. This ends the moment I leave."

Key Points

  • Show the beautiful logo and mockups.
  • Explain that this creates a consistent, premium feel across all platforms.
  • Silence. Let her look at her new brand. Wait for her to say 'I love it.'
Once she says she likes the logo, say: "Great, let's get the first month started so I can send these over to you today."
