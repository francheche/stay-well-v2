A Traveler's Guide to Wellness
in Clark Freeport Zone

Navigating the **Clark Freeport Zone** as a business traveler or tourist often means tight schedules, long flights into Clark International Airport (CRK), and high-pressure meetings. Maintaining your physical and mental well-being is crucial to staying productive and enjoying your stay. This guide provides practical tips for achieving elite wellness during your visit.

1. Beat Jet Lag Immediately

The first 24 hours after landing at CRK are critical. To reset your internal clock, we recommend a high-intensity **Deep Tissue Recovery** session. Deep pressure helps flush out the lactic acid and toxins accumulated during long flights, while improving lymphatic drainage to reduce post-flight swelling.

2. Choose Your Sanctuary Wisely

Clark is home to some of the finest hotels in the Philippines, including the **Marriott, Swissotel, Hilton, and Midori**. While these establishments offer incredible amenities, many guests prefer the privacy and convenience of an in-room treatment. Stay Well Massage specialize in delivering a full-service spa experience directly to these hotels, ensuring you don't have to navigate lobby traffic after your session.

3. Active Recovery for Golfers

For those visiting Clark for its world-class golf courses (like Mimosa or Sun Valley), muscle stiffness is a common challenge. Traditional **Thai Restorative Massage**—often called "lazy yoga"—is the ideal choice for golfers. The assisted stretching and rhythmic compression help restore joint mobility and alleviate tension in the shoulders and lower back.

4. The Importance of Professionalism

In a mobile service environment, safety and ethics are paramount. Always ensure your wellness provider is DOH-licensed. At Stay Well, we maintain a **Strict 100% No Extra Service Policy**, providing a safe and therapeutic environment for all travelers, including female solo travelers and families.
