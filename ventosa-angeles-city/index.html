Traditional Ventosa Cupping Therapy.

Ancient Healing for Modern Tension

Ventosa, or traditional cupping therapy, is an ancient healing art that has been used for centuries to relieve pain and promote circulation. At Stay Well Massage, we bring this powerful treatment directly to your home or hotel in Angeles City, combining it with professional therapeutic massage for maximum effectiveness.

How Ventosa Works

Unlike massage, which applies pressure to the muscles, Ventosa uses suction to pull tension away from the body. Our DOH-licensed therapists use specialized glass cups to create a vacuum on the skin's surface. This suction increases blood flow to stagnant areas, helping to flush out metabolic waste and reduce inflammation in the back and shoulder regions.

Ventosa is particularly effective for those suffering from "lamig" or trapped air in the muscles, chronic back stiffness, and congestion. It is a key component of our Signature Recovery package, designed specifically for travelers and residents who need more than just a surface-level massage.
