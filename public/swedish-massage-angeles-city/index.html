The Therapeutic Benefit

Using long, gliding strokes (effleurage) and rhythmic kneading (petrissage), our DOH-licensed therapists focus on comfort and perceived muscle tension. Massage may offer short-term relaxation for some people, but it does not remove toxins and is not a treatment for anxiety, insomnia, or another medical condition.

Swedish vs Thai Massage: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Swedish massage if you prefer an oil-based session with long, gliding strokes and rhythmic kneading. Choose Thai massage if you prefer compression and assisted stretching. Pressure and movement are adjusted to your comfort, and you can confirm the best option when booking.
