Traditional Ventosa Cupping Therapy

Traditional Cupping for Modern Tension

Ventosa, or traditional cupping, is a long-used practice that applies suction cups to the skin. Some guests choose it for perceived muscle tension, but research is limited and temporary circular marks are common. We offer Ventosa at homes and hotels in Angeles City as an optional addition to a professional massage session.

How Ventosa Works

Unlike massage pressure, Ventosa uses suction on the skin. Our DOH-licensed therapists apply cups for a controlled period and explain expected temporary marks and aftercare. Evidence for pain and other health benefits is limited, and cupping can cause burns, skin irritation, infection, or persistent discoloration if performed improperly.

Ventosa is particularly effective for those suffering from "lamig" or trapped air in the muscles, chronic back stiffness, and congestion. It is a key component of our Signature Recovery package, designed specifically for travelers and residents who need more than just a surface-level massage.
